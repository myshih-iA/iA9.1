Complete Operations // Sustainment // Global Market Delivery

Mission-Critical Forward Ops & Lifecycle Support

iA’s Ops+ service layer turns marketplace access and trusted sourcing into real-world execution. Through strategic logistics partners, iA provides the backend infrastructure needed to move UxV components, subsystems, and platforms from suppliers to customers — with the support required for deployment, sustainment, and scale.

Our capabilities include warehousing, inventory management, fulfillment, secure global shipping, testing coordination, light assembly, RMA, reverse logistics, technical support routing, hazmat material handling, Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ)-related support, and potential manufacturing or integration support through qualified partners.

This service layer addresses a critical gap in the UxV ecosystem: many suppliers can build strong products, but buyers also need reliable delivery, local support, spare parts, repair pathways, compliance documentation, and lifecycle sustainment. iA helps convert suppliers from overseas vendors into serviceable, U.S.-ready and global partners.
