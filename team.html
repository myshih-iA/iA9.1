Chief Operating Officer

Matt Mueller
BS, MBA

Matt Mueller is a seasoned leader in technology operations, advanced manufacturing, and system reliability. At Honeywell Aerospace, he spearheaded development testing for the Airbus A350 program. He then spent nine years at SpaceX building out core quality management processes—driving down defect rates to accelerate the production, testing, launch, and refurbishment of Falcon 9 vehicles. Most recently, as Head of Quality at Hadrian, Matt earned the company its AS9100 and ISO 9001 certifications.
