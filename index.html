iA is a platform centered on US-Taiwan cooperation, connecting global demand, providing trusted supply chain integration, transactions, and full product lifecycle services.

We combine Taiwan's industrial strengths in precision manufacturing, electronics, semiconductors, sensors, batteries, communications, and system integration with real market demands in the U.S. and worldwide. We assist suppliers from market entry to transactions, logistics, RMA, localized execution, and end-to-end product lifecycle services—building a more transparent, resilient, and scalable supply chain.

iA collaborates with manufacturers and system providers. We deliver the critical execution layer between supply capacity and market landing—connecting real-world demand, trusted supply, and global execution through Market Access, Trusted Marketplace, Operations & Logistics, AI-Native Intelligence, and Financial Layer solutions.

iA 是以台美合作為核心、串聯全球需求、提供可信任供應鏈整合、交易、與全產品生命週期服務平台

我們結合台灣在精密製造、電子、半導體、感測器、電池、通訊與系統整合的產業優勢，串聯美國與全球實際市場需求，協助供應商從市場進入、提供交易，物流、RMA、在地執行與全產品生命週期服務，建立更透明、具韌性且可規模化的供應鏈。

iA 與製造商以及系統供應商合作；我們提供從供應能力到市場落地之間最關鍵的執行層，並透過 Market Access、Trusted Marketplace、Operations & Logistics、AI-Native Intelligence 與 Financial Layer，連結真實需求、可信任供應與全球執行能力。
