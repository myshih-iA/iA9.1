About iA

Mission // Vision // Resilient Supply Chains // Democratic Alliances

Enabling Scalable and Resilient Production for UxV Systems

iA bridges advanced autonomous-system design with industrial-scale execution. Through trusted marketplace access, vetted democratic supply chains, production-ready workflows, and Ops+ services, we help dual-use partners source, scale, and deploy reliable uncrewed platforms with resilient supply chains.

Our core business is to help buyers source reliable components, subsystems, and integrated solutions through a supplier network verified through strict due diligence and globally coordinated.

// Core Model

Capability & Capacity Aggregation

At the center of our model is capability and capacity aggregation. iA identifies what suppliers can build, how much they can produce, how quickly they can scale, and how reliably they can support customers across air, ground, surface, and undersea UxV applications.

By aggregating these capabilities across Taiwan and other trusted global sources, iA helps customers access a stronger and more flexible supply base.

// Quality Assurance

Verified Supplier Due Diligence

Each supplier and product package is reviewed for company background, ownership structure, sourcing pathway, technical documentation, production capability, capacity, compliance readiness, and supply-chain risk.

The goal is to provide buyers with more than a product listing — we provide a verified information package that supports procurement, compliance review, logistics planning, and deployment decisions.

// Integrated Infrastructure

Multi-Layer Operating System

iA built a multi-layer supply chain operating system for UxV and autonomous systems. We connect trusted suppliers, verified products, marketplace demand, logistics execution, financial transactions, lifecycle sustainment, and AI-native intelligence into one integrated platform.

Our model is designed to solve the “hard middle” of the autonomous systems ecosystem: not only finding products, but making them sourceable, verifiable, deliverable, serviceable, financeable, and scalable across U.S. and allied global markets.

// Dynamic Scaling & Continuous Optimization

The AI-Native Flywheel

Every transaction, supplier interaction, shipment, repair, replacement, market signal, and field update creates new data. iA uses that data to continuously improve the platform and supply-chain decisions.

These insights help identify which products are in demand, which suppliers are most reliable, where bottlenecks exist, what should be stockpiled, and where alternative sources are needed. The result is a smarter, scalable, and more resilient supply chain platform that improves over time.
